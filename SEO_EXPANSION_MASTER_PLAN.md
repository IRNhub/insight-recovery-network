## Executive answer

IRN has a solid crawlable base and a strong authority layer, but it does not yet have the commercial substance-treatment layer required to turn that authority into qualified treatment enquiries. The live XML sitemap contains 83 indexable URLs: 44 resource articles, 11 commercial landing pages, 6 service/treatment pages, 8 assessment pages including the hub, 5 trust/policy pages, 4 about/E-E-A-T pages, and 5 other core pages. Every sitemap URL returned HTTP 200, a unique title, a meta description, a self-canonical and exactly one H1 in the 28 August crawl.

The highest-value next move is not a new `/treatment/` directory or hundreds of location pages. It is a set of six clearly differentiated commercial treatment-selection pages connected to the already-live alcohol, cocaine, cannabis, ketamine, benzodiazepine and dual-diagnosis authority pillars, with `/treatment-placement` retained as the broad addiction-treatment hub. This plan implements that first batch and reserves detox, cost, comparison, geographic and international expansion for controlled follow-on releases.

## Evidence and scope

This audit reconciles four sources:

- the current production sitemap and the raw HTML returned for all 83 URLs;
- route, metadata, schema, redirect, sitemap, robots and prerender logic in the current remote repository;
- non-sitemap probes for noindex utilities, legacy redirects, trailing slashes, alternate hosts and true 404 behavior;
- the existing 28-article authority programme and the 11 August topical-authority roadmap, which remain the authority layer and are not replaced by this plan.

Search Console and keyword-platform data were not connected. Priorities are therefore directional and should be re-scored when impressions, clicks, average position, CTR, conversions and query-level evidence are available.

## Internal-linking model

Every authority pillar should link to one supporting cluster page, its relevant commercial treatment page, one decision/cost/location page when useful, and one suitable assessment/enquiry route. Commercial pages should link back to the authority pillar and onward to the appropriate detox, cost, comparison and assessment pages. Natural anchor variation is required; repeated exact-match anchors are not.

Example: `/resources/cocaine-addiction` → `/cocaine-addiction-treatment` → `/resources/cocaine-withdrawal` → `/how-much-does-rehab-cost-uk` → `/resources/online-addiction-support-vs-residential-rehab` → `/assessments/drug-use` or `/get-help`.

## Clinical and editorial gates

- IRN may assess needs, support recovery decisions and navigate or place clients with appropriate providers. It must not claim to diagnose, prescribe, supervise withdrawal or directly provide inpatient/medical detox unless a separately verified service scope changes.
- Alcohol, benzodiazepine and opioid withdrawal pages require named clinical review and prominent medical/emergency boundaries.
- Ketamine content must separate addiction support from medical assessment of urinary or other physical harm.
- Dual-diagnosis, trauma and ADHD pages must avoid self-diagnosis and clearly identify when specialist or emergency care is outside IRN's role.
- Cost ranges require a currency, date, inclusion/exclusion notes and a verification owner. Do not invent partner prices.
- Every health page should expose author, relevant credentials, original/updated date, reviewer where applicable, sources and a medical-information disclaimer.
- Use NHS, NICE, GOV.UK/OHID, ONS and primary/peer-reviewed research. Do not use competitor copy as evidence.

## Visual-content gate

Every new or substantially improved page must receive an asset-level visual audit before publication. Record the current hero and supporting images, pixel dimensions, aspect ratios, placements, ALT text and Open Graph reference; then decide separately whether a dedicated hero is needed, supporting editorial photography would add comprehension or trust, and an existing IRN asset is genuinely suitable for reuse.

Visual inspection is mandatory. Do not approve an image from its filename or ALT text alone, and do not assume that a generic site image is sufficient. Reuse must be rejected where an asset duplicates a different pillar, misstates the visible scene, contains readable fabricated material, relies on a promotional brand panel or conflicts with the page’s clinical scope.

Where a bespoke asset is needed, write an 11-field page-specific image brief covering URL, purpose, scene, people/objects, composition, tone, UK context, IRN palette/style, crop, ALT text and exclusions. The SEO expansion workflow must not use Replit AI, external AI image generation or automatic stock sourcing. Default delivery is a 1600 × 900 WebP hero or body image plus a separate 1200 × 630 Open Graph crop, with no baked-in text and a safe central crop area.

The tracker fields `Hero image status`, `Supporting image status`, `Image brief status`, `ALT text status` and `OG image status` are release controls, not optional notes.
