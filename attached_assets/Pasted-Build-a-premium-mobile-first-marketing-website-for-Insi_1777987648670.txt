Build a premium, mobile-first marketing website for Insight Recovery Network.

This is Phase 1 of the project. The goal is to create a classy, calm, high-end public website that does not look generic or AI-generated. It should feel like a trusted private clinical network, not a cheap rehab funnel, not a template site, and not a busy health-tech dashboard.

Business context:
Insight Recovery Network provides confidential addiction and mental health support, including private treatment placement, online recovery programmes, family/intervention guidance, and Insight OS, a digital recovery platform.

Primary goals:
1. Build trust quickly.
2. Position Insight Recovery Network as premium, private, clinical and human.
3. Guide visitors into three clear pathways:
   - I need help for myself
   - I need help for someone else
   - I am a professional or organisation
4. Capture enquiries through a clean contact form.
5. Create a strong foundation for later phases: assessments, blog, CRM integration, Meta Pixel, SEO content and Insight OS integration.

Tech requirements:
- Use React, Vite, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- Use clean reusable components.
- Use responsive mobile-first design.
- Use accessible semantic HTML.
- Do not use overly flashy animations.
- Add subtle premium transitions only.
- Keep the project structure simple and easy to expand.
- Use placeholder images for now, but structure the design so hero and section images can be swapped easily.
- Do not connect any backend yet unless there is already a simple local form placeholder.
- Contact form can show a “Thank you, we’ll be in touch shortly” confirmation state for now.

Brand style:
The design should feel:
- Calm
- Private
- Premium
- Clinically credible
- Human
- Warm
- Minimal
- Spacious
- Reassuring

Avoid:
- Generic AI-generated gradients everywhere
- Stock-photo-heavy rehab clichés
- Bright medical blues
- Overcrowded sections
- Excessive icons
- Cheap sales-funnel styling
- Overuse of cards
- Loud animations
- Robotic copy
- Anything that feels like a SaaS landing page template

Visual direction:
- Use a sophisticated palette:
  - Deep navy / charcoal as the main dark tone
  - Warm ivory or soft stone as the page background
  - Muted gold or champagne as a subtle accent
  - Soft sage or muted teal may be used sparingly
- Typography should feel editorial and premium.
- Use large spacing, strong hierarchy and refined section layouts.
- Buttons should be clean, subtle and confident.
- Rounded corners should be elegant, not cartoonish.
- Use soft shadows very sparingly.
- Make it look excellent on mobile.

Suggested navigation:
- Home

- What We Offer
- Treatment Placement
- Online Programme
- Insight OS
