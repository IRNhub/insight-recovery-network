Do not change scoring values or scoring order. Only improve the user-facing answer labels where needed.

Use these standard scales:

1. Frequency questions:
- Every day or almost every day
- Most days
- Some days
- Rarely or not at all

Use this for questions asking “how often”.

2. Impact/severity questions:
- A lot
- Moderately
- A little
- Not at all

Use this for questions asking how much something affects daily life, relationships, work, sleep, mood, functioning, or wellbeing.

3. Control/difficulty questions:
- Very difficult
- Quite difficult
- A little difficult
- Not difficult

Use this for questions about managing, controlling, stopping, cutting down, or resisting urges.

4. Agreement questions:
- Strongly agree
- Somewhat agree
- Not sure
- Disagree

Use this for belief/reflection statements.

5. Duration questions:
Use plain time ranges, for example:
- More than a year
- 6–12 months
- 1–6 months
- Less than a month

6. Safety/risk questions:
- Yes
- Sometimes
- No
- Prefer not to say

Use this for risk, safety, self-harm, withdrawal, medical, or safeguarding questions.
