We need to audit and fix the SEO crawlability and AI visibility issues on insightrecoverynetwork.com.

Multiple live routes appear to be exposing very similar crawler-visible HTML, including /assessments, /treatment-placement, /insight-os, /what-we-offer, /about and /contact.
