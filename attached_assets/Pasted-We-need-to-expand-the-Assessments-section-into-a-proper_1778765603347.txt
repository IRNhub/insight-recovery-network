3. Result Bands

Use result bands such as:

Low concern:
Responses suggest no immediate high-risk pattern, but the user may still benefit from education, tracking, or Insight OS.

Moderate concern:
Responses suggest patterns worth discussing with a professional. Recommend booking a confidential call or exploring the online programme.

Elevated concern:
Responses suggest a higher level of concern. Recommend professional assessment and a confidential call with Insight Recovery Network.

Urgent/detox concern:
For detox-related risk responses, recommend speaking with a medical professional urgently and contacting Insight Recovery Network for guidance. Avoid giving medical instructions.

4. Safety and Clinical Boundaries

Do not diagnose.
Do not tell people they are addicted.
Do not provide detox instructions.
Do not give medication advice.
Do not imply emergency support is available 24/7 unless we explicitly offer that.
Use language like:
“Your answers suggest…”
“This may indicate…”
“It may be worth speaking with a professional…”
