# IRN Assessment Platform Phase B instrument decision

Date: 30 August 2026
Status: implementation record, pending legal and Clinical Director approval

## Decision

The four Phase B assessments use clearly labelled IRN-developed descriptive needs profiles. They do not reproduce, approximate, score or claim to be WHO AUDIT or WHO ASSIST. No combined IRN total is produced.

This is the authorised fallback in the Phase B brief. It preserves independent safety triage and leaves the definition contract ready for a later permission-confirmed validated instrument.

## WHO AUDIT

Authoritative sources reviewed:

- [WHO AUDIT publication page](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-MSD-MSB-01.6a)
- [WHO AUDIT manual PDF](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2017-06/english_0.pdf)
- [WHO copyright policy](https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/copyright)
- [WHO permissions guidance](https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/permissions)

The WHO manual describes the recognised 10-item AUDIT, its scoring and its interviewer or self-administered use. The manual's historic distribution statement does not establish permission for reproduction in IRN's current commercial, company-sponsored website context. WHO's current permissions guidance requires a permissions process for commercial use. IRN has not supplied written WHO permission for this implementation.

Therefore:

- the questionnaire wording and recognised score are not reproduced;
- the result is not labelled AUDIT;
- AUDIT worked-example and score-boundary tests are not fabricated;
- later AUDIT integration remains permission-gated.

## WHO ASSIST

Authoritative source reviewed:

- [WHO ASSIST publication page and manual](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/978924159938-2)

The WHO source presents ASSIST as a primary-care manual for health professionals and reserves reproduction rights. Permission and administration requirements for public, commercial self-administration by IRN were not conclusively established. No written permission was supplied.

Therefore:

- ASSIST wording and scoring are not reproduced;
- no "ASSIST-like" score is used;
- the Drug Use assessment is a substance-specific IRN needs profile;
- later ASSIST integration remains permission and administration-gated.

## Approval gates

Before either instrument can be activated, IRN should retain written permission or other documented legal authority, confirm the intended administration model, obtain Clinical Director approval, implement the instrument as a separately identified score, and add official worked-example and boundary tests from the authorised source.
