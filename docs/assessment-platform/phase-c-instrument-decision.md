# Phase C validated instrument and adult eligibility decision

Date: 30 August 2026

Status: instrument use confirmed; IRN context, safety mappings, deterministic findings and pathways approved by the Clinical Director on 30 August 2026 following the final clinical correction pass.

This clinical approval is not legal advice, privacy or data-protection approval, regulatory approval, medical-device classification, production-release approval, or a substitute for validating the cited instrument conditions for IRN's exact live use.

## Decision

| Assessment | Implemented instrument | Decision basis | Implementation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anxiety | GAD-7, seven items, 0-21 | Pfizer states that its PHQ screeners are available through PHQ Screeners and do not require a formal permission request when the applicable terms are followed. Pfizer also publicly released PHQ and GAD-7 tools without copyright restriction or charge. | The seven published items, response options and 0-3 scoring are reproduced. IRN context, safety answers and pathways do not alter the GAD-7 score. |
| Depression | PHQ-9, nine items, 0-27 | Same Pfizer permission position as above. | The nine published items, response options and 0-3 scoring are reproduced. PHQ-9 item 9 contributes to the PHQ-9 total and independently enters the safety engine. It is never presented as a suicide-risk score. |
| ADHD | Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS-v1.1) six-question screener, original shaded-box 0-6 method | Harvard's National Comorbidity Survey states that the six-question screener may be used without formal permission if the required citation and copyright notice are included and the response options, scoring and shaded thresholds are not altered. | The six published questions and five response options are reproduced. Official threshold responses remain visually distinguished. Four or more threshold responses means the screening threshold is reached. A screening result cannot diagnose ADHD. |

## Adult eligibility

All three Phase C assessments are explicitly adult-only. An age question appears before any instrument item. Selecting under 18 displays age-appropriate GP and urgent mental-health pathways, hides the adult questionnaire, disables submission in the browser and is rejected by server-side validation if forged.

No paediatric instrument, score or interpretation has been invented. The adult-only clinical boundary and under-18 pathway are clinically approved. Legal, privacy, safeguarding-process and UK-nation applicability review remain required before production activation.
